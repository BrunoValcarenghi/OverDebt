# OVERDEBT

An atmospheric 1-bit action platformer made for [1-Bit Jam 15](https://itch.io/jam/1-bit-jam-15), inspired by the theme **Everything Has a Price**.

Descend into a hostile sector, fight through increasingly dangerous enemies, and collect scrap to improve your gear. Make it back to base with your haul: if your health or energy reaches zero, you lose all the scrap you are carrying.

Every upgrade helps, but power has a cost. Stronger shots, faster fire, and a wider firing angle consume more energy per shot. Improving your speed or energy capacity also makes your suit drain faster over time. Choose what to buy, keep an eye on your resources, and decide when it is time to turn back.

## How to Play

- Fight enemies and collect the scrap they drop.
- Return to the central base before your health or energy runs out.
- Use the computer to buy and improve your upgrades. Their scrap prices increase with each level.
- Use the energy terminal at base to recharge your suit.
- The deeper you go, the more dangerous the enemies become.

## Controls

| Action | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or Left / Right Arrow |
| Jump | Space, W, or Up Arrow |
| Aim | Hold Right Mouse Button while grounded |
| Shoot | Hold Left Mouse Button while aiming |
| Interact / recharge / advance dialogue | E |
| Buy an upgrade | Click an upgrade on the computer |

## The Jam

OVERDEBT was made for [1-Bit Jam 15](https://itch.io/jam/1-bit-jam-15), whose theme is **Everything Has a Price** and whose main condition is to use two colors. The game explores that theme through risky resource runs, permanent upgrade choices during a run, and the scrap lost when a run ends.
