1-BIT ACTION PLATFORMER

OVERDEBT

EVERYTHING HAS A PRICE

Descend. Survive. Return with your scrap.

YOU OWE MORE THAN YOU CAN PAY

Your suit is failing. The sector below is crawling with enemies, and scrap is the only way to improve your chances.

Descend into the sector, fight your way through, and decide how long you can risk staying. Make it back to base with your haul. If your health or energy reaches zero, every scrap you are carrying is lost.

EVERY UPGRADE HAS A PRICE

Spend scrap at the base computer to improve your:

  • Jump height and movement speed
  • Damage and firing rate
  • Health and energy capacity
  • Shot angle and invincibility time

Each level costs more scrap. Stronger shots, faster firing, and a wider aim angle consume more energy. Speed and energy capacity also make your suit drain faster over time.

CONTROLS

MOVE — A / D or Left / Right Arrow

JUMP — Space, W, or Up Arrow

AIM — Hold Right Mouse Button while grounded

SHOOT — Hold Left Mouse Button while aiming

INTERACT — E to recharge, advance dialogue, or return to base

UPGRADE — Click an upgrade on the base computer
